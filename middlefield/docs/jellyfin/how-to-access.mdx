# How to access

Jellyfin is available at **`https://jellyfin.middlefield.xyz`**. You'll need the account credentials I've given you to sign in — reach out if you don't have them yet.

You can watch and listen from a web browser, or install a dedicated app for a better experience (offline downloads, casting, background audio, etc.). For movies and TV shows, use **Moonfin**. For music, use the app recommended for your platform — it's built specifically for music and works better than a general video app for that.

## Web browser

No install needed — works on any device with a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).

1. Go to [https://jellyfin.middlefield.xyz](https://jellyfin.middlefield.xyz)
2. Sign in with your username and password
3. Start browsing your libraries

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't connect?** Double check the server address is exactly `https://jellyfin.middlefield.xyz` (include `https://`).
- **Buffering or stuttering?** This is usually a transcoding issue — try lowering playback quality in the app's settings, or ask me to check the server.
- **Forgot your password?** Reach out and I can reset it for you.
